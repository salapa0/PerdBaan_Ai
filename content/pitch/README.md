# content/pitch

**What goes here:** everything for presenting, not for the app:
- The business slide: pricing tiers, value logic (THB 490/month costs less than one extra booked night), break-even, sponsor model
- The demo script: what we say and click, step by step
- A backup screen recording of the full demo, in case something fails live
- A "Next steps" slide for what we did not build

**Who:** C (content and data).

Competition rule to remember: the pitch must show it is about tourism and that it can make money.
